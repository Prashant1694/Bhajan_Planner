— Bhagwan Sri Sathya Sai Baba

Raga (Melody)

Sweet, uplifting notes in faithful alignment with authentic mandir scales.

Taala (Rhythm)

Synchronized steady beat uniting the whole congregation as one prayer.

Singer’s Code of Conduct & Mandir Discipline

Punctuality
Singers seated and ready before Veda recitation concludes.
Harmonious Seating
Instrumentalists and lead vocalists positioned for seamless eye-contact.
3-Speed Format
1st speed twice, 2nd speed once (last line twice), 3rd speed drut tempo.
Pitch & Sruti
Select a scale comfortable for the entire hall to chant along effortlessly.
Synchronized Claps
Rhythmic hand clapping dispels dullness and elevates devotional vibration.
Silence & Sanctity
Phones on complete silent mode; uphold tranquility within the mandir hall.
